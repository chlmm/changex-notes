# 餐厅点餐

## 场景描述

你在一家英语餐厅。需要找位子、点菜点饮料、提特殊要求、最后结账。

## 关键句型

### 进店与入座

| 正式度 | 表达 |
|--------|------|
| 随意 | "Table for two, please." |
| 中性 | "Hi, we'd like a table for two." / "Do you have a table for two?" |
| 正式（有预约） | "Good evening. I have a reservation under [Name]." |

### 被问到几位时

| 表达 |
|------|
| "Just me." / "Two of us." / "There'll be four of us." |

### 换座位

| 正式度 | 表达 |
|--------|------|
| 随意 | "Can we sit by the window?" |
| 中性 | "Could we get a table by the window, if possible?" |

### 点菜

| 正式度 | 表达 |
|--------|------|
| 随意 | "Can I get the burger?" / "I'll have the pasta." |
| 中性 | "I'd like the grilled salmon, please." / "Could I have the Caesar salad?" |
| 请对方推荐 | "What do you recommend?" / "What's popular here?" |
| 点饮料 | "I'll have a coffee, please." / "Can I get a glass of water?" |

### 特殊要求

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 过敏 | "I'm allergic to nuts. Does this dish contain any?" |
| 饮食偏好 | "Do you have any vegetarian options?" / "Is this gluten-free?" |
| 调整菜品 | "Could I get that without onions?" / "Can I have the dressing on the side?" |
| 牛排熟度 | "I'll have it medium-rare, please." (rare / medium / medium-well / well-done) |

### 用餐过程中

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 叫服务员 | 眼神接触 + 点头，或轻轻抬手 |
| 需要东西 | "Excuse me, could we get some napkins?" / "Could I get another fork?" |
| 夸赞菜品 | "This is really good." / "Everything's great, thanks." |
| 劝菜/分享 | "Help yourself!" / "Try some of this." / "Have some more." |

### 结账与离开

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 要账单 | "Could we get the check, please?" / "Can I have the bill?" |
| 分开付 | "Can we split it?" / "Could we do separate checks?" |
| 请客 | "It's on me." / "I've got this." / "Let me get this one." |
| 离开时 | "Thanks so much, everything was great." / "We really enjoyed it." |

### 你可能会听到的（服务员/店员常说的话）

| 你听到的 | 意思 | 怎么回应 |
|---------|------|---------|
| "How may I assist you today?" | 服务场景的标准问候 | "I'd like a table for two, please." 或直接说你的需求 |
| "Say when." | 倒酒/上菜时说"够了就告诉我" | 够了就说 "That's good, thanks." 或 "That's perfect." |
| "Are you ready to order?" | 问我可以点菜了吗 | "Not yet, thanks." / "Yes, I think we're ready." |
| "Enjoy your meal." | 用餐愉快 | "Thank you!" |
| "How is everything?" / "Is everything okay?" | 问我吃得怎样 | "Great, thanks." / "Everything's wonderful." / 如有问题："Actually, could I get..." |
| "Would you like to see the dessert menu?" | 要看甜点菜单吗 | "Sure, thanks." / "No thanks, just the check please." |

### 小费文化

- **美国**：通常 15–20%，不给或给很少会被认为很不礼貌
- **英国/欧洲**：服务费通常已包含，额外给 5–10% 即可
- **澳大利亚/日本**：不需要给小费

## 对话范例

### 范例1：随意午餐

> **Host**: Hi, welcome! How many?
>
> **You**: Just two, please.
>
> **Host**: Right this way. Here's your table. Your server will be right with you.
>
> **Server**: Hey there! Can I get you started with some drinks?
>
> **You**: I'll have an iced tea, please. And what do you recommend for lunch?
>
> **Server**: The fish tacos are really popular. The club sandwich is also great.
>
> **You**: I'll go with the fish tacos, please. No cilantro though, if that's okay.
>
> **Server**: Sure thing! And for you?
>
> **Friend**: I'll have the club sandwich, thanks.
>
> **Server**: Great, I'll be right back with your drinks.

### 范例2：正式餐厅

> **Host**: Good evening. Do you have a reservation?
>
> **You**: Yes, it's under Chen. Party of two.
>
> **Host**: Of course, Mr. Chen. Right this way, please.
>
> **Server**: Good evening. May I start you off with something to drink?
>
> **You**: I'll have a glass of the house red, please. And could I see the menu for a moment?
>
> **Server**: Of course, take your time. I'll be right back with your wine.
>
> *(稍后)*
>
> **You**: Excuse me, I'm ready to order. I'd like the grilled salmon, please. Is it possible to have the sauce on the side?
>
> **Server**: Absolutely. And for your starter?
>
> **You**: The Caesar salad, please — no croutons, I'm gluten-free.
>
> **Server**: Not a problem at all. I'll put that in for you.

## 文化提示

- **绝对不要喊 "Waiter!"** ——眼神接触或轻轻抬手就行，等服务员经过时说 "Excuse me" 即可。
- **"Check"（美式）和 "Bill"（英式）** 都能听懂，但入乡随俗更好。
- **美国餐厅分量通常很大**，打包很正常："Could I get a box for this?"
- **等服务员带你入座** ——除非有 "Seat yourself" 的牌子，不要自己随便坐。
- **和服务员闲聊** 在美国很常见——一句 "How's your day going?" 既友好又得体。

## 常见错误

| ❌ 不要这样说 | ✅ 应该说 | 原因 |
|-------------|-----------|------|
| "I want the steak" | "I'd like the steak, please" | "I want" 听起来像命令 |
| "Give me the menu" | "Could I see the menu, please?" | 更礼貌自然 |
| 大喊 "Waiter!" | 眼神接触或说 "Excuse me" | 大声叫人在西方被认为很粗鲁 |
| "I am ready"（准备点菜时） | "I think we're ready to order" / "We're ready when you are" | "I am ready" 太生硬 |
| "How much?"（要结账时） | "Could we get the check, please?" | 应该要账单，而不是直接问价格 |
| 在桌上说 "It's too expensive" | 等离开后再评论 | 在桌上抱怨价格不礼貌 |
