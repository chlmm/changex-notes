# 问候与自我介绍

## 场景描述

你遇到了一个人——在派对上、办公室里、校园里，或者只是路过。你需要打招呼、可能要自我介绍，最后说再见。

## 关键句型

### 打招呼

| 正式度 | 表达 |
|--------|------|
| 随意 | "Hey!" / "Hi there!" / "How's it going?" |
| 中性 | "Hi, how are you?" / "Good to see you." |
| 正式 | "Good morning." / "It's a pleasure to meet you." |

### 别人问 "How are you?" 时怎么回

| 正式度 | 表达 |
|--------|------|
| 随意 | "Pretty good!" / "Not bad." / "Can't complain." |
| 中性 | "I'm doing well, thanks. And you?" |
| 真诚回答（对朋友） | "Honestly, a bit tired." / "Been better, but okay." |

### 自我介绍

| 正式度 | 表达 |
|--------|------|
| 随意 | "I'm [Name], by the way." / "Hey, I'm [Name]." |
| 中性 | "Hi, I'm [Name]. Nice to meet you." |
| 正式 | "Allow me to introduce myself — I'm [Name]." / "I'm [Name]. It's a pleasure." |

### 介绍他人

| 正式度 | 表达 |
|--------|------|
| 随意 | "This is my friend [Name]." |
| 中性 | "I'd like you to meet [Name]." / "Have you met [Name]?" |
| 正式 | "May I introduce [Name]?" |

### 告别

| 正式度 | 表达 |
|--------|------|
| 随意 | "See ya!" / "Catch you later." / "Take care!" |
| 中性 | "It was nice talking to you." / "Have a good one." |
| 正式 | "It was a pleasure meeting you." / "I look forward to seeing you again." |

## 对话范例

### 范例1：派对上认识新朋友

> **A**: Hey, I don't think we've met. I'm Alex.
>
> **B**: Hi Alex, I'm Jordan. Nice to meet you!
>
> **A**: Nice to meet you too. So, how do you know the host?
>
> **B**: We're coworkers. You?
>
> **A**: College friends. Small world!
>
> **B**: For real! Well, it was great meeting you, Alex. I'm gonna grab a drink.
>
> **A**: Yeah, you too! See you around.

### 范例2：正式商务介绍

> **A**: Good morning. I'm Alex Chen from the Singapore office.
>
> **B**: Good morning, Mr. Chen. I'm Jordan Taylor. It's a pleasure to finally meet you in person.
>
> **A**: The pleasure is mine. I've heard a lot about your team's work.
>
> **B**: Thank you. Shall we head to the conference room?
>
> **A**: Of course, lead the way.

## 文化提示

- **"How are you?" 通常只是问候**，不是真在问你的状态。除非是好朋友，否则简短正面回答即可。
- **眼神交流和微笑** 比你选哪个词更重要。
- **握手** 在商务场合是标准动作。坚定（但不是使劲捏）+ 眼神交流，是西方文化中的规范。
- **"Nice to meet you"** 用于初次见面；已经认识的人用 **"Nice to see you"**。
- **称呼**：随意场合很快就会用名。正式场合等对方先用你的名称呼你，或者说 "Please, call me [名]"。

## 常见错误

| ❌ 不要这样说 | ✅ 应该说 | 原因 |
|-------------|-----------|------|
| "What is your name?"（生硬地问） | "I'm [Name]. And you?" | 直接追问姓名显得突兀 |
| "I am called [Name]" | "I'm [Name]" / "My name is [Name]" | "I am called" 不自然 |
| "How do you do?"（除非非常正式） | "How are you?" / "Nice to meet you." | "How do you do?" 在大多数场合已经过时 |
| 朋友之间说 "Goodbye" | "See you!" / "Bye!" / "Take care!" | "Goodbye" 对朋友太正式了 |
| 别人问 "How are you?" 时长篇大论诉苦 | 简短回答，然后反问 | 一见面就抱怨会让气氛尴尬 |
