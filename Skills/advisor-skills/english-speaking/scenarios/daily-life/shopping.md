# 购物

## 场景描述

你在英语国家的商店、超市或商场购物。需要找商品、问价格、试穿、付款，以及可能的退换货。

## 关键句型

### 进店与寻物

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 店员主动招呼 | "Hi, can I help you?" / "Are you finding everything okay?" |
| 你只是看看 | "Just browsing, thanks." / "I'm just looking, thanks." |
| 找具体商品 | "Excuse me, do you have [item]?" / "Where can I find the [item]?" |
| 问尺码 | "Do you have this in a medium?" / "Do you have this in a size [X]?" |
| 问颜色 | "Does this come in any other colors?" |

### 问价格与预算

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 问价格 | "How much does this cost?" / "How much is this?" |
| 看了价格嫌贵 | "That's a bit out of my budget." / "That's more than I was looking to spend." |
| 问是否有折扣 | "Is this on sale?" / "Are there any discounts on this?" |
| 问退税（旅游时） | "Can I get a tax refund on this?" |

### 试穿与决定

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 要求试穿 | "Can I try this on?" / "Where are the fitting rooms?" |
| 试穿后合适 | "It fits perfectly." / "This looks great." |
| 试穿后不合适 | "It's a bit tight/loose." / "It doesn't quite fit." |
| 决定不买 | "I'll think about it, thanks." / "I'll pass for now." |

### 付款

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 准备结账 | "I'd like to pay for this, please." |
| 支付方式 | "Do you accept credit cards?" / "Can I pay by card?" / "I'll pay cash." |
| 要袋子 | "Could I get a bag, please?" |
| 要收据 | "Could I get a receipt, please?" |

### 退换货

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 要求退货 | "I'd like to return this, please." |
| 要求换货 | "Could I exchange this for a different size/color?" |
| 说明原因 | "It doesn't fit." / "It was a gift and it's not quite right." / "I found a defect here." |
| 店员可能问 | "Do you have the receipt?" / "When did you purchase this?" |
| 有收据时 | "Yes, here's the receipt." |
| 没收据时 | "I don't have the receipt, unfortunately. Is that okay?" |

### 超市常用

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 找过秤区 | "Where can I weigh this?" |
| 找收银台 | "Where's the checkout?" |
| 要不要袋子 | "Would you like a bag?" → "Yes, please." / "No thanks, I have my own." |
| 自助结账 | "The self-checkout is over there." |

## 对话范例

### 范例1：服装店购物

> **店员**: Hi there, can I help you find anything?
>
> **你**: Yes, I'm looking for a lightweight jacket. Do you have anything like that?
>
> **店员**: Sure, they're right this way. What size are you?
>
> **你**: Medium, usually.
>
> **店员**: Here you go. The fitting room is just over there.
>
> *(试穿后)*
>
> **你**: It fits pretty well. How much is it?
>
> **店员**: It's $89. And we actually have a 20% off promotion today.
>
> **你**: Great, I'll take it. Do you accept credit cards?
>
> **店员**: We do. Here's your receipt and your bag. Have a great day!
>
> **你**: Thanks, you too!

### 范例2：超市结账

> **收银员**: Did you find everything okay today?
>
> **你**: Yes, thanks. Oh, do you have bags, or should I use my own?
>
> **收银员**: We have paper bags for 10 cents each.
>
> **你**: I'll take one, please. Can I pay by card?
>
> **收银员**: Of course. Just tap or insert right here. ... All set. Here's your receipt.
>
> **你**: Thanks, have a good one.

### 范例3：退货

> **你**: Hi, I'd like to return this shirt, please.
>
> **店员**: Sure. Do you have the receipt?
>
> **你**: Yes, here it is. I bought it last week, but it doesn't fit right.
>
> **店员**: No problem. Would you like a refund or an exchange?
>
> **你**: A refund, please.
>
> **店员**: Alright, I'll process that for you. It should be back on your card within 3–5 business days.
>
> **你**: Sounds good, thanks.

## 文化提示

- **"Just browsing" 是非常常见的回应** — 不需要觉得不好意思，店员习惯了。
- **价格标签就是最终价格**（美国除外）— 在美国，标价不含消费税，结账时会加上。欧洲/澳洲/亚洲的标价通常已含税。
- **小票很重要** — 退换货几乎都需要收据，养成保留小票的习惯。
- **试衣间一次可以拿多件** — 通常没有数量限制，但不买的放回原位或交给店员处理即可。
- **自助结账很普遍** — 在欧美超市，自助结账机是常态，不需要和收银员对话。

## 常见错误

| ❌ 不要这样说 | ✅ 应该说 | 原因 |
|-------------|-----------|------|
| "Give me this one" | "I'll take this one, please." | "Give me" 听起来像命令 |
| "How much money?" | "How much is this?" / "How much does it cost?" | "How much money" 不自然 |
| "I want to return my money" | "I'd like a refund, please." | 前者语法不对，且听起来生硬 |
| "Can I change this?" | "Can I exchange this?" | "Change" 在购物语境中指找零，不是换货 |
| 不带小票去退货 | 先问 "I don't have the receipt — is there anything you can do?" | 很多商店没收据也能退，但态度要软 |
