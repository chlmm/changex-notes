# 面试

## 场景描述

你在参加一个工作面试——线下或线上。需要自信地介绍自己、回答行为类和技术类问题、向面试官提问、并专业地结束。

## 关键句型

### 开场

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 问候 | "Hi, it's great to meet you. Thank you for having me." |
| 多位面试官 | "Hello everyone, nice to meet you all." |
| 线上面试 | "Thanks for making time for this. Can you hear me okay?" |

### 自我介绍（"Tell me about yourself" 问题）

| 部分 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 开头（现在） | "I'm currently a [role] at [company], where I focus on..." |
| 中间（经历） | "Before that, I worked at [company] doing..." / "Over the past [X] years, I've specialized in..." |
| 结尾（连接职位） | "What excites me about this position is..." / "I'm looking to take the next step in [area], and this role feels like a great fit." |

**结构建议**：现在 → 过去 → 未来。先说你现在在做什么，简要回顾经历，然后连接到为什么来面试。

### 用 STAR 法回答行为类问题

当被问到 "Tell me about a time when..." 或 "Give me an example of..."

| 部分 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| **S** 情境 | "In my previous role, we were facing..." |
| **T** 任务 | "My responsibility was to..." / "I was tasked with..." |
| **A** 行动 | "What I did was..." / "I took the initiative to..." / "I collaborated with [team] to..." |
| **R** 结果 | "As a result, we..." / "The outcome was..." / "This led to a [X]% improvement in..." |

### 应对难题

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 不知道答案 | "That's a great question. I don't have the exact answer off the top of my head, but my approach would be..." |
| 经验有缺口 | "While I haven't done [X] directly, I have experience in [related area] which I believe transfers well." |
| 弱点问题 | "An area I've been working on is... For example, I've started [action] to improve." |
| 为什么离职 | "I've learned a lot at my current role, but I'm looking for [growth/opportunity/challenge] that this position offers." |

### 反问面试官（"Any questions for us?" 时）

| 关注点 | 表达 |
|--------|------|
| 关于职位 | "What does a typical day look like in this role?" |
| 关于团队 | "Can you tell me about the team I'd be working with?" |
| 关于成长 | "What does career progression look like here?" |
| 关于文化 | "How would you describe the team culture?" |
| 关于期望 | "What would success look like in the first 90 days?" |
| 收尾问题 | "Is there anything about my background that gives you hesitation?" |

### 结束面试

| 情况 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| 表达兴趣 | "After our conversation, I'm even more excited about this opportunity." |
| 强调匹配 | "I think my experience in [area] would really help me contribute from day one." |
| 致谢 | "Thank you for your time. I really enjoyed our conversation." |
| 了解后续 | "What are the next steps in the process?" |

### 跟进邮件

面试后 24 小时内发送：

> Subject: Thank you — [Your Name]
>
> Hi [Interviewer's Name],
>
> Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about the [Position] role. I really enjoyed learning more about [specific topic discussed].
>
> Our conversation reinforced my interest in the position. I'm confident that my experience with [relevant skill/project] would allow me to contribute to the team.
>
> Please don't hesitate to reach out if you need any additional information. I look forward to hearing from you.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]

## 对话范例

### 范例1：开场与自我介绍

> **面试官**: Hi Alex, thanks for coming in. Let's start — tell me a bit about yourself.
>
> **你**: Sure! I'm currently a front-end developer at TechCorp, where I've been for about three years. Before that, I worked at a startup building customer-facing dashboards. Over time, I've developed a strong focus on performance optimization and accessibility. What excites me about this role is the chance to work on a product that reaches millions of users — that scale really appeals to me.
>
> **面试官**: Great, thanks for that overview. Let me ask you about a time you had to deal with a tight deadline.

### 范例2：回答行为类问题

> **面试官**: Can you tell me about a time you had a conflict with a teammate and how you resolved it?
>
> **你**: Absolutely. In my last project, a designer and I disagreed on the implementation approach for a key feature — I wanted a simpler solution, and they pushed for a more complex design.
>
> **面试官**: And how did you handle it?
>
> **你**: I set up a one-on-one meeting to understand their perspective better. Turns out, the complexity was driven by a user need I hadn't fully considered. We ended up finding a middle ground that addressed the user need without over-engineering the solution. The feature launched on time and got great feedback. I learned that sometimes slowing down to listen actually speeds things up.
>
> **面试官**: That's a great example. I appreciate the self-reflection.

### 范例3：结束时的反问

> **面试官**: Alright, that covers most of what I wanted to ask. Do you have any questions for me?
>
> **你**: Yes, a couple. First — what does success look like in the first six months for this role?
>
> **面试官**: Great question. We'd want someone to ship their first major feature within the first quarter, and by six months, be mentoring junior developers.
>
> **你**: That sounds like a great pace. And one more — how would you describe the team culture?
>
> **面试官**: Very collaborative. We do pair programming a couple times a week, and there's a strong emphasis on code reviews.
>
> **你**: That's exactly the kind of environment I'm looking for. Thanks for sharing that.

## 文化提示

- **自信但不自大** — 客观陈述你的成就。"I led the project" 没问题，"I single-handedly saved the company" 就过了。
- **可以想一下再答** — 如果需要思考，说 "That's a great question, let me think about that for a second。" 比胡乱说好。
- **一定要提问** — 结束时没有问题会被认为不感兴趣。提前准备 3–5 个，用 2–3 个。
- **跟进邮件** — 24 小时内发一封简短的感谢信是西方求职的标配。
- **诚实比完美重要** — 不知道就说不知道，然后说明你会怎么找到答案。编造答案远比承认缺口糟糕。
- **线上面试**：提前测试设备。说话时看摄像头而不是屏幕。穿着和线下面试一样正式。

## 常见错误

| ❌ 不要这样说 | ✅ 应该说 | 原因 |
|-------------|-----------|------|
| "I have no weaknesses" | "An area I've been improving is..." | 每个人都有弱点，否认听起来很假 |
| "I don't know"（然后沉默） | "I don't have that info right now, but I'd approach it by..." | 即使不知道，也要展示解决问题的思路 |
| "What does your company do?" | 根据你的研究问具体问题 | 这说明你没做准备 |
| "I just need a job" / "I need the money" | 聚焦于为什么想要*这个*职位 | 雇主想看到真正的兴趣 |
| "My current boss is terrible" | "I'm looking for new challenges" | 绝对不要说前任/现任雇主的坏话 |
| "I'm a perfectionist"（当被问弱点时） | 给一个真实的、不太严重的弱点 + 你的改进计划 | 这是最陈词滥调的回答，毫无价值 |
| 结束时没有任何问题 | 至少准备 2–3 个有深度的问题 | 表示你真的感兴趣 |
